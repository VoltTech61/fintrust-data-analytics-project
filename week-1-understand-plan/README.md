# Week 1 – Understand & Plan

Week 1 focused on understanding the FinTrust business problem and the available customer and transaction data before beginning the actual analysis.

## Week 1 Activities

- Reviewed the FinTrust business problem
- Profiled the customer and transaction datasets
- Identified data-quality observations
- Examined the relationship between the datasets
- Developed analytical questions
- Proposed KPIs
- Designed a dashboard wireframe
- Created an initial analysis plan for Weeks 2–4

## Dataset Overview

- 1,500 customer records
- 12,000 transaction records
- Customer_ID links the customer and transaction tables
- Customer_ID is unique while Customer_Name is not unique
- Missing values were identified in Device_Type and Location
